Taza on any website

One line of code adds a round button in the corner of the page. Its colour and number are the air in Almaty right now; tapping it opens a chat with AAI's research, with exact quotes and page links. This page shows the real widget, as a website visitor would see it (look at the bottom-right corner).

Options: data-lang="en", "ru" or "kk" (default: the page's own language), data-position="left". To allow only your site to embed it, set WIDGET_ORIGINS=https://air.org.kz on the server.
